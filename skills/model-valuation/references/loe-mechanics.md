# LOE & Generic/Biosimilar Entry Mechanics — forecasting reference

Companion to MOD-04 (LOE bridge) and SUB-PHA-05 (biosimilar erosion). Seed figures compiled Aug 2026 from secondary reviews of FDA records — tag `(as of Aug 2026 — verify at use)` and confirm against Orange Book/Purple Book, the ANDA approvals docket, and litigation disclosures when a forecast drives a position.

## Generic-entry timing — forecast from the public docket

- **The forecasting tool:** cross-reference Orange Book patent/exclusivity expiries against the **tentative-approval docket**. Tentative approvals are queued competition — ANDAs approved-but-blocked by patent/exclusivity/30-month stays; the queue length at an expiry date predicts the cliff's steepness. Same-day multi-sponsor final-approval clusters mark first-generic cliffs (the dapagliflozin pattern: 7+ sponsors on one day).
- **Timing anchors:** brand→first-generic lag has run ~5–6 years post-launch for recent large launches; ANDA filing→tentative approval ~10–14 months; total lock-in windows 18–48 months. **180-day exclusivity** for the first Para-IV filer shapes year-one erosion (single-generic pricing before the cliff).
- **BE bounds:** generic bioequivalence = 90% CI of AUC and Cmax within **80.00–125.00%** — the mechanical bar; complex generics (injectables, devices co-packaged, peptides) fail on CMC/device grounds more than BE.
- **Erosion shapes:** small molecules 80–90% revenue loss in ~18 months; see below for biologics.
- **Peptide scope note:** ANDA generics exist only for a short named list (glucagon, liraglutide, nesiritide, teriparatide, teduglutide as of Aug 2026); impurity/immunogenicity control is the moat — GLP-1 generic timing runs through this gate.
- **DMF/GDUFA telegraphy:** API drug-master-file activity and GDUFA completeness assessments (~6 months pre-ANDA) telegraph generic-entry intent before filings are public.

## Biologics — the 351(k) lane

- **Routing:** proteins >40 amino acids are biologics; since the 2020 deemed-BLA transition, follow-ons come via **351(k)** (biosimilar), not 505(b)(2) — track the **Purple Book**, not the Orange Book. Biosimilar applications can file at year 4 of the reference product's 12-year exclusivity.
- **Approval odds framework:** the three-tier analytical-similarity structure — Tier 1 equivalence testing (90% CI within margin) for the most critical quality attributes, Tier 2 quality ranges (mean ± ~3 SD), Tier 3 visual — with a three-way US/EU bridge standard. Precedent: attribute misses can be rescued by functional data (the Inflectra ~20% FcγR-binding gap approved with functional justification) — analytical perfection is not the bar; totality-of-evidence is.
- **Erosion shape:** 30–50% over 3–5 years typical; interchangeability designations, contracting/rebating, and channel (medical vs pharmacy benefit) set the pace — model contracting, not just science.

## CGT tail liabilities (rNPV inputs)

Gene/cell-therapy approvals carry structural post-approval costs to model explicitly: **15-year long-term follow-up registries** (≥1,500 patients each for the CAR-T class; final reports due ~2042), clonality/integration-site monitoring triggers, post-approval **class boxed warnings** added years after launch (CAR-T T-cell-malignancy 2024 pattern), and accelerated-approval confirmatory risk (the Elevidys/EMBARK primary-endpoint miss — price the confirmatory event as its own gate). These are per-program fixed costs and probability haircuts that generic rNPV templates omit.

## Post-approval change tiers (supply/site risk)

Annual report < CBE-30 < **PAS** per the magnitude of the change: site moves, sourcing changes, and shelf-life extensions at the PAS tier are approval-gated, dated events — a manufacturing-flexibility constraint worth checking in supply-disruption scenarios (pairs with clinical-catalysts quality-signals).
