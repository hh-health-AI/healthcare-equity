# Healthcare & Life Sciences Commercial Metrics (master glossary)

From `Healthcare_and_Life_Sciences_Commercial_Metrics.xlsx` (project file) — volume, adoption, and performance metrics across the four sub-sector ladders. Sector slices also ship in the engine plugins (payor → cms-reimbursement; adoption → provider-adoption; volume/funnel → procedure-exposure).

## Pharmaceuticals & Biotechnology

| Metric | Full name | Role | Definition & strategic meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| NRx | New Prescriptions | New demand / flow | New prescriptions written and filled. Tracks market capture, launch velocity, physician adoption, campaign impact. |
| TRx | Total Prescriptions | Total volume / stock | All prescriptions filled (NRx + refills). Primary proxy for top-line revenue and overall market share. |
| NBRx | New-to-Brand Prescriptions | Sub-category of NRx | Patients on the brand for the first time. Isolates true organic market growth from renewals. |
| Refill Rate | Refill volume (TRx − NRx) | Adherence | Patient compliance, long-term retention, chronic-therapy continuation. |

## Life Science Tools & Diagnostics

| Metric | Full name | Role | Definition & strategic meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Placements / Net Placements | Instrument placements | "NRx equivalent" | New capital instruments installed in the period (gross minus decommissioned/returned). |
| Installed Base (IB) | Active installed base | "TRx equivalent" | Cumulative active instruments in the field — the foundation for high-margin recurring revenue. |
| Pull-Through | Consumable pull-through | Utilization / unit economics | Recurring consumable/reagent revenue per installed instrument per year ($/instrument/yr). Tracks lab utilization. |
| Attachment Rate | Service/consumable attach rate | Cross-sell / retention | % of instrument sales bundled with service contracts or proprietary assay/reagent kits. |

## MedTech & Medical Devices

| Metric | Full name | Role | Definition & strategic meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Procedure Volume | Total surgical/diagnostic procedures | "TRx equivalent" | Procedures performed using the device in the period. |
| Reorder / Utilization Rate | Account reorder rate | "NRx equivalent" / retention | Frequency and volume of hospital reorders of single-use disposables after trial usage. |
| System Placements | Capital placements | Capital flow | New capital units (robots, imaging) sold, leased, or placed via disposable-commitment agreements. |
| ASP | Average Selling Price | Pricing power | Average realized price per unit — monitors discounting, competitive erosion, lifecycle margin. |

## Managed Care & Health Insurance

| Metric | Full name | Role | Definition & strategic meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lives / Covered Lives | Total membership | "TRx equivalent" | Active subscribers across Commercial, MA, Medicaid — the master volume base. |
| MLR / MBR | Medical Loss / Benefit Ratio | Margin / underwriting | Claims ÷ premium revenue; primary profitability gauge (target ~80–85%). |
| PMPM | Per Member Per Month | Normalized unit | Standard unit for revenue and medical cost per enrollee per month. |
| Net Organic Additions | Net membership growth | "NRx dynamics" | New enrollees minus disenrollments in enrollment periods. |

## Usage rules

Pick the ladder that matches the sub-sector; never mix flow (NRx, placements, net adds) with stock (TRx, installed base, lives) in one growth claim; pair every volume metric with its economics link (refill rate, pull-through, ASP, PMPM) before calling growth "quality"; and note disclosure changes — a KPI a company stops disclosing is itself a signal (EARN-02 rule).
