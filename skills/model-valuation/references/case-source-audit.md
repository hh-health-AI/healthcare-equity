## Corrections and limits

- **C01:** Company topline results only; the full paper, statistical analysis plan (SAP) and FDA decision have not been verified. Reported major adverse cardiovascular event (MACE) intervals cross 1, so they do not establish cardiovascular benefit.
- **C02:** Injection time is not the full visit time. PK evidence does not establish superior antitumor efficacy.
- **C03:** The retrieved abstract gives a 35% relative reduction in the mortality hazard, rather than 36%. Original Q2 commercial and guidance claims were removed; they were not certified. Review of the full paper, SAP and current label remains outstanding.
- **C04:** Access, prescriptions and paid courses are different quantities. No current paid-fill ratio is inferred.
- **C05:** Keep the format approval and label expansion dates distinct. Detailed clinical outcomes have not been independently audited.
- **C06:** Average active-system denominators have not been established, so no utilization per system is calculated. The showcase verifies additional FY 2025 baseline fields.
- **C07:** The issuer reports 283 randomized participants and 265 completing 26 weeks. The full paper and SAP were not reviewed. Do not describe the baseline change as the treatment difference.
- **C08:** Reported tests are not necessarily paid tests or collected revenue. Volume growth uses distinct test definitions; the accrual change is not recurring cash ASP.
- **C09:** Clinical scope only. Earlier Q2 financial and cash-burn sources are not certified by this review.
- **C10:** This statement concerns bioprocessing, not companywide orders. Conversion and cancellations remain model assumptions.
- **C11:** The old static URL failed and was replaced with the currently linked official prepared remarks. Output refers to connected instruments, not all installed systems.
- **C12:** Awards are already net of cancellations. Do not subtract cancellations twice or describe the quarterly ratio as LTM or YTD.
- **C13:** The continuing CDMO business excludes CHI. The CORE measure differs from the reported EBITDA margin of 34.1%.
- **C14:** Revenue per equivalent admission includes Florida directed payments. Companywide and same-facility measures do not form one exact bridge.
- **C15:** This release alone does not establish drug pass-through as the cause. The gross margin is a derived calculation.
- **C16:** Do not describe all development as prior-year development. A mechanical add-back is not a pure current-quarter service-cost ratio. The showcase retains the EPS rounding caveat.
- **C17:** The 25% figure is guidance, not observed growth. No numerical spread between new and retained cohorts was verified.
- **C18:** The unsupported claim that GLP-1 dilution explains this quarter was removed. Percentage-margin effects remain model hypotheses.
- **C19:** Claims use the issuer's 30-day-equivalent definition. Preserve the table and narrative conventions; these observations do not establish profit per claim.
- **C20:** This release does not separately quantify paid AI adoption. The absence of that metric is scoped to this document.
- **C21:** Prescribers are not all providers. NRR is a TTM measure; cash and EBITDA changes are year over year. Usage is not monetization.
- **C22:** Do not describe Q2 growth as H1 growth. The share metric is issuer analysis of third-party data and was not independently measured here.
- **C23:** US species growth differs from global species growth. Specific causal assertions about visits and outbreaks were removed from the checked evidence statement.
- **C24:** Installed-base growth is not placement growth. Keep reported and organic revenue measures separate.

## Model and reopening discipline

Model mappings in the [case library](case-library.md) remain hypotheses. An evidence update must use CHANGE, NO_CHANGE, UNCERTAINTY_ONLY or NEEDS_DATA and retain the model assumption, period, units, rationale and next observable. Checking a reported fact does not justify an automatic forecast change.

Reopen a statement when a filing is corrected, a source link changes, a newer reporting period is substituted, or the population, denominator or endpoint changes. Record the new primary document and exact locator before changing the scoped status.

Three [reproducible research demonstrations](../../../examples/research-cases/README.md) extend selected evidence into explicit calculations. They use illustrative forecasts and conditional market-expectation frameworks; they are not priced, fully underwritten recommendations.
