## Verified scope

- **C02**: FDA approval on 2025-09-19; Study MK-3475A-D77 randomized 377 patients; dual primary endpoints assess PK exposure. This does not establish superior efficacy.
- **C05**: Company reports 20–30-second self-injection after training and expansion to all adult gMG serotypes.
- **C06**: Q2 2026 combined procedures grew 16%, da Vinci procedures 15%, Ion procedures 36%; da Vinci ending installed base grew 12%; I&A revenue grew 18%. These do not establish utilization growth per average active system.

## Corrections and reopening criteria

- C03: the 28% HELIOS-B figure concerns a composite endpoint; it must not be labeled mortality alone. The original issuer URLs currently redirect to a release index, so the full case remains pending.
- C06: removed the derived ~3% utilization growth. The numerator combined platforms and the denominator was a single-platform ending base; use matching platform procedures and average active systems.
- C08/C09: contribution deducts processing costs for all processed tests, including unpaid tests.
- C12: distinguish gross awards from net awards; subtract cancellations only once. Do not substitute YTD book-to-bill for LTM.
- To promote a case: open the exact primary document, confirm reporting period and units, record section/page/table and the checked statement, reconcile conflicts, then change the ledger status. Model mappings remain analyst hypotheses even when the underlying fact is verified.
