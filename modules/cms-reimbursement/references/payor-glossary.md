# Payor Glossary — the terms that appear in analysis

Distilled for equity-research use. For textbook depth, `project_search` the project KB (*Dictionary of Health Insurance and Managed Care*; *Health insurance and managed care*) when attached to the Healthcare Plugin project.

## Money flow & margin
- **MLR / MBR (Medical Loss / Benefit Ratio)** — medical claims ÷ premium revenue; the payor P&L's dominant line. Statutory minimums (ACA): 80% individual/small group, 85% large group and MA — excess triggers rebates.
- **MCR (Medical Cost Ratio)** — synonym used by several issuers; "reserve-normalized current-year MCR" is the scenario discriminator for managed care (see metrics-payor.md).
- **PMPM / PMPY** — per member per month/year; the normalized unit for both premium and cost.
- **Member-month** — the payor economic unit: profit = member-months × premium PMPM × (1 − MCR − admin ratio).
- **Prior-period development (PPD)** — revision of prior-quarter claim reserves; favorable development flatters reported MLR and must be normalized out.
- **IBNR** — incurred-but-not-reported claim reserves; the estimate whose revisions become PPD.
- **Days claims payable (DCP)** — claims reserve ÷ daily claims expense; falling DCP can flag reserve release supporting earnings.
- **Admin ratio / operating cost ratio** — SG&A ÷ revenue; the other margin lever.

## Medicare Advantage mechanics
- **Benchmark** — county-level bidding ceiling derived from FFS spending; the Advance/Final Rate Notice moves it.
- **Bid & rebate** — plans bid against the benchmark; bidding below returns a rebate (% set by Star rating) funding extra benefits.
- **Star Ratings** — CMS quality scores (released October); ≥4 stars earns quality bonus payments and larger rebates ~2 years forward; contract-level, so concentration matters.
- **Risk adjustment / HCC / V28** — diagnosis-driven revenue scaling (RAF scores); the V28 model revision compresses coding-driven revenue, phased in across payment years.
- **RADV audits** — CMS clawback audits of risk-adjustment coding; a tail liability for MA issuers.
- **AEP / OEP** — annual (Oct 15–Dec 7) and open (Q1) enrollment periods; membership swing windows.
- **Normalization factor / effective growth rate** — Rate Notice components that, netted, set benchmark growth.

## Part D & drug channel
- **Part D redesign** — post-IRA benefit: $2,000 OOP cap, manufacturer discounts in initial/catastrophic phases, plan liability up sharply in catastrophic.
- **Maximum Fair Price (MFP)** — the negotiated Medicare price for selected drugs; measured against net, not list.
- **Gross-to-net (GTN)** — list-to-net waterfall: rebates, discounts, 340B, coverage-gap/redesign liabilities.
- **340B** — statutory deep discounts to covered entities; a growing GTN drag and a hospital-margin subsidy.
- **PBM economics** — rebate retention vs pass-through, spread pricing, specialty pharmacy capture; profit ≈ claims × net profit/claim + specialty/services.

## Coverage & utilization control
- **NCD / LCD / coverage article** — national vs MAC-local Medicare coverage policies and their billing guidance.
- **MAC** — Medicare Administrative Contractor; regional claims processor issuing LCDs.
- **CED (Coverage with Evidence Development)** — coverage conditional on registry/trial participation; common for novel devices.
- **Prior authorization / step therapy / formulary tier** — utilization-management levers; friction that separates covered lives from paid demand.
- **Site-of-service policy** — steering procedures to cheaper settings (HOPD → ASC → office); reprices the same volume.
- **NTAP / TPT** — temporary add-on payments for new technology (inpatient / outpatient); bridge economics before codes and rates mature.

## Structures
- **FFS vs capitation vs shared savings** — the risk continuum from fee-for-service to full-risk (VBC); risk-bearing providers earn the premium-cost spread.
- **MSSP / ACO / REACH** — Medicare shared-savings vehicles.
- **Vertical integration** — payor-owned PBM/provider assets (UNH/Optum pattern); intercompany eliminations obscure segment economics.
- **Medicaid managed care / redeterminations** — state-contracted plans; eligibility churn drives membership and acuity mix.
- **Duals / D-SNP** — dual-eligible (Medicare+Medicaid) special-needs plans; high-acuity, high-PMPM books.
