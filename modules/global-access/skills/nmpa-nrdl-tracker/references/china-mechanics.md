# China access mechanics

## The three payment routes, which behave completely differently

1. **NRDL (National Reimbursed Drug List).** Annual negotiation, concluding late in the
   year, effective the following January. Large negotiated price reductions in exchange
   for nationwide reimbursement and hospital listing. Volume response is typically fast
   and large. The renewal negotiations in later years matter as much as the first entry,
   because renewal terms can cut price again.
2. **VBP (volume-based procurement).** Centralised tendering for off-patent molecules,
   and progressively for devices, consumables and IVD reagents. Price outcomes are far
   more severe than NRDL, and the mechanism transfers volume to whoever wins the tender.
   This is the dominant risk for mature portfolios and for medtech.
3. **Self-pay and commercial insurance**, including the city-level supplementary schemes.
   Growing, but small relative to the state channel for most products.

## Modelling discipline

- Never model an NRDL entry as a price event alone. The trade is price for volume and
  hospital access; model both, and state the volume elasticity assumed.
- Hospital listing is a real, separate step after NRDL entry. A product on the list but
  not stocked in hospitals does not sell.
- Provincial variation is material for both timing and volume.
- For renewals, check whether the product's actual sales exceeded the forecast agreed at
  entry, which affects the renewal terms.

## The licensing flow

China-origin clinical-stage assets are being licensed out to Western developers in large
numbers and at scale. For the desk, this matters in three ways:

1. **As a valuation comparator.** Each deal sets an observable price for a stage and a
   modality. A Western biotech whose value rests on a preclinical or Phase 1 asset now
   competes with a cheaper, faster alternative source of the same thing.
2. **As a pipeline input.** Large-cap pharma pipeline replenishment increasingly runs
   through in-licensing, which changes the return profile of internal R&D.
3. **As a policy-exposed flow.** BIOSECURE-type legislation and export controls could
   restrict it. Track legislative status, not commentary, and treat the outcome range
   as wide.

Extract for every deal: upfront cash, milestones and total biodollars, equity component,
territory split (whether Greater China is retained), development stage at signing, and
royalty structure. Headline biodollars are the least informative of these and the most
quoted.

## Sources

NMPA and CDE publish approvals, designations and acceptances in Chinese;
chinadrugtrials.org.cn carries the trial registry. NRDL results are published by the
National Healthcare Security Administration. Where a finding rests on a translation, say
so and cite the original.
