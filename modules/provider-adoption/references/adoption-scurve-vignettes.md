# Adoption S-Curves in Medicine — pattern library

Distilled patterns from the history of interventional cardiology and device diffusion (see *The Heart Healers* and *Commercializing successful biomedical technologies* in the project KB via `project_search` for the narratives). Use these to place a product's adoption evidence on the curve and name the gate ahead of it.

## The recurring sequence

1. **Maverick/evangelist stage.** One or a few clinician-innovators adopt against establishment skepticism, often at a handful of academic centers; evidence is case series and registries. Adoption is fragile and personality-driven. *Investment read:* footprint confined to trial/KOL centers; growth can stall on one adverse series or the loss of an evangelist.
2. **Contested-evidence stage.** Randomized trials arrive; specialty societies argue; complication management matures (technique, training, patient selection). *Read:* watch trial outcomes and guideline-committee signals — this stage sets whether the curve inflects or dies (plenty of celebrated techniques regressed here).
3. **Guideline & payment stage.** Positive RCTs → guideline inclusion → coding/coverage/payment falls into place → training programs scale. This is where the S-curve's steep middle begins — and it is *institutional*, not clinical: the gate is reimbursement and training capacity (check cms-reimbursement for the payment gate, procedure-exposure for code maturity).
4. **Mainstream diffusion.** Community (non-trial) sites adopt; volumes shift from academic to community settings; competitors enter with iterations; price/mix pressure starts. *Read:* diffusion beyond the trial-site base is the strongest durable-demand signal (kol-site-map's third bucket).
5. **Maturity & displacement.** The installed technique becomes the incumbent a new S-curve attacks (surgical → percutaneous → transcatheter is the canonical cardiology ladder). *Read:* today's beneficiary is tomorrow's disruptee; track the next curve's stage-1 evangelists.

## Gate checklist (what stops curves between stages)

Evidence gate (RCT vs registry) · guideline position · reimbursement (code + coverage + payment adequate to site economics) · training/proctoring burden and learning curve · usability and workflow fit (see usability-adoption-checklist.md) · capital cost and site certification · complication visibility (one publicized failure mode can stall a curve for years).

## Quantifying stage from footprint data

Evangelist: ≲ dozens of sites, high academic skew, flat curve. Inflection: quarter-over-quarter net site adds accelerating, community share of new adds rising. Mainstream: net adds decelerating while volume/site (procedure-exposure) still climbs — the base saturates before utilization does. Maturity: site base flat/declining, growth only from utilization/mix.
