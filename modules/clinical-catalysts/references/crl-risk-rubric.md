# CRL-Risk Rubric — scoring a pending application

A checklist for handicapping approval decisions (NDA/BLA/PMA/supplements). Seed patterns compiled Aug 2026 from secondary reviews of FDA CRLs, approval packages, and enforcement records — verify against primary sources at use. Used by the adcom-label skill's pre-decision checklist; pairs with `base-rates.md` and `quality-escalation-ladder.md`.

## The deficiency taxonomy (rank-ordered)

CRLs catalog every deficiency at once; most letters cite multiple categories. Empirical ordering (as of Aug 2026): **CMC/product quality is the most common category, and ~12 of 20 sampled CRLs cited facility/CGMP/site-compliance status** — facility readiness is an approval axis orthogonal to clinical strength. The eight families to score:

1. CMC / product quality (stability, impurities, container-closure/E&L, sterility assurance)
2. Facility / CGMP / inspection status (see the escalation ladder — OAI at a named site is close to disqualifying)
3. Clinical efficacy (endpoint failure, non-generalizable population, statistical fragility)
4. Clinical safety (signal outweighing benefit; inadequate exposure database)
5. Nonclinical gaps
6. Clinical pharmacology / dosing (post-Optimus: absent randomized dose comparison in oncology)
7. Bioequivalence / data integrity (terminal when integrity is the issue)
8. Labeling / REMS (near-boilerplate — rarely sole cause, often riders)

## Named red flags with precedent patterns

Score each present flag; two or more high-severity flags → CRL is the base case, not the tail.

- **Subgroup rescue** — overall trial failed, approval sought on a post-hoc subgroup: near-certain CRL; the historical remedy is a *prospective* confirmatory trial in the subgroup (BiDil→A-HeFT; Radicava→dedicated confirmatory). Expect a 12–24-month reset.
- **Hy's Law case counts** — ALT/AST >3×ULN with bilirubin >2×ULN: one case is worrisome, two are highly predictive of DILI risk (~10% mortality reference); FDA has rejected monitoring-only mitigations (tolebrutinib, vadadustat CRL precedents). Count the cases in the safety database.
- **Ex-US-heavy / single-country pivotal data** — post-2024 MRCT guidance, China-only oncology packages have drawn CRLs for non-generalizability (2025 examples; sintilimab 2022 is the anchor precedent). Check US enrollment share and any pre-pivotal FDA alignment on it.
- **MTD-only phase-1 oncology dosing** — Project Optimus (final guidance Aug 2024) expects randomized multi-dose comparison before registrational trials; MTD-only designs are a live deficiency channel.
- **Late CMC qualification on parenterals/biologics** — container-closure integrity methods without stated LOD, E&L programs started late, missing in-process limits: recurring CRL and PMC drivers.
- **PPQ not complete at filing** — process-performance-qualification promised for the 120-day update rather than filed: recurring facility-adjacent deficiency.
- **Named-facility enforcement status** — any application-listed site under OAI, import alert, or unresolved warning letter (score via quality-signals); "not ready for inspection" alone has produced CRLs.
- **Missing/unfinished confirmatory apparatus for AA** — FDORA requires confirmatory trials underway at approval.
- **Novel-modality blinding/durability problems** — the Lykos/MDMA CRL template: functional unblinding unmeasured, expectancy effects uncontrolled, durability follow-up too short, therapy component not isolated. Reusable rubric for any subjectively-active drug.

## Aftermath mechanics (for the post-CRL trade)

A CRL closes the review cycle and lists all deficiencies; the sponsor has ~1 year to resubmit or withdraw (inaction = withdrawal); partial responses are not processed. Resubmission classification (Class 1 ≈ 2-month, Class 2 ≈ 6-month clocks) and a promptly-granted post-CRL Type A meeting are the remediation-clarity signals. Facility-only CRLs can resolve on reinspection timelines independent of clinical review.

## Output form

Score → per-category verdict (clean / at-risk / red flag, each with cited evidence) → overall CRL-probability adjustment vs the pathway base rate → the 1–2 falsifiers to watch (inspection completion, ODAC scheduling, labeling-negotiation signals). End with the EVIDENCE BRIEF block.
