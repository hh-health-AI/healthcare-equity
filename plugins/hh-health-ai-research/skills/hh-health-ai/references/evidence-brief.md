# Evidence brief template

```text
EVIDENCE BRIEF
Question / entity:
Layer: clinical | regulatory | commercial | competitive | financial
Finding: observed result, with source and precise locator
Dates: publication/effective date; retrieval date; reporting period/data vintage
Coverage: sources and exact queries; record/document counts; caps; known gaps
Implication: CHANGE | NO_CHANGE | UNCERTAINTY_ONLY | NEEDS_DATA
Assumption: variable; prior value -> proposed value, or explicitly unavailable
Rationale: why that implication follows; what is already reflected in the model
Not automatic: inferences this observation does not license
Follow-up: next observable that would confirm or refute the interpretation
Confidence: qualitative assessment, optionally 0.00–1.00 with explanation
```

For purely biomedical questions, do not force a financial model onto the answer. Replace investment implications with the answer's applicability and uncertainty. For cross-module work, give each finding one evidence identifier and reuse it without counting the same signal as independent evidence.

Suggested evidence packet fields: source_id, title, URL or supplied-file locator, document_type, publication_date, effective_date, retrieval_time, record_id, period_or_vintage, query, retrieved_count, total_reported, cap, completeness_scope, claim, short_supporting_passage, locator, assessment, caveats. Preserve legal access restrictions. Use short quotations only when needed and permitted.
