# Methodology

Appraisal dimensions: randomization and allocation concealment; masking; estimand and intercurrent-event strategy; superiority/noninferiority hypothesis; comparator suitability; power assumptions; interim looks and alpha spending; endpoint hierarchy; missing-data sensitivity; analysis sets; adverse-event denominators and exposure; subgroup interaction tests; external validity.

A p-value does not measure effect size or clinical importance. Nonsignificance is not equivalence. An unadjusted subgroup p-value is not evidence of treatment-effect heterogeneity. Noninferiority depends on the margin and assumptions. Cross-trial comparisons cannot establish superiority. In oncology distinguish ORR, DOR, PFS and OS and note maturity and censoring. Do not reconstruct individual patient data from a graph without documenting digitization uncertainty.

Output includes document dates and passages. Missing data remain null, not zero. Use the evidence skill for claim-level traceability. The comparison utility checks field equality only; the agent must inspect every flagged difference.
