# Methodology

Ten reusable procedures:
1. PICO: translate an ambiguous question into population, intervention/exposure, comparator and outcomes; add setting/time.
2. PubMed search: combine MeSH where valid with free-text synonyms; preserve exact translated query; pilot against known relevant papers.
3. Study appraisal: choose design-appropriate bias domains (randomization, deviations, missingness, outcome measurement, selection of reporting); do not assign an automatic GRADE rating.
4. Trial interpretation: extract estimand, effect measure, baseline risk, absolute and relative effects, confidence interval, multiplicity and missing-data strategy.
5. Document reconciliation: compare registry history, protocol/SAP, paper/supplement and release; establish version dates before alleging changes.
6. Evidence table: unit is study/cohort/outcome/timepoint, not merely publication. Link multiple reports to the same study.
7. Claim verification: test whether the passage entails the exact population, intervention, outcome and certainty in the claim.
8. Conflicting evidence: examine design, power, population, dose, follow-up and risk of bias; do not decide by paper count.
9. Journal club: select the eight-slide briefing workflow for teaching and discussion.
10. Plain-language explanation: retain uncertainty and distinguish relative from absolute effects. Do not convert population evidence into individual advice.

Downgrade certainty for inaccessible methods, selective reporting, imprecision, indirectness, confounding or inconsistency. Quantitative pooling requires compatible estimands and an explicit analysis plan; the renderer does not perform meta-analysis.
