# Methodology

Search design: controlled vocabulary plus synonyms; no unexamined language/date restrictions; pilot sensitivity with known studies; registry and citation searching where relevant; version exact query strings. PubMed does not cover all preprints, conference evidence or unpublished trials.

Deduplication: normalize DOI prefix/case, match PMID, preserve original records and removed-to-retained mapping. If identifiers conflict, retain all for adjudication. Matching titles are candidates, not automatic duplicates. Do not collapse a preprint and final paper without checking study identity and changes.

Screening: separate automation suggestions from final decisions and retain reasons. A two-reviewer process requires actual independent reviewers; do not claim it occurred if only one host agent screened. Quantitative meta-analysis, risk-of-bias judgments and certainty grading require explicit methods and are not supplied by the deduplication script.
